# Courrier design direction

## Product surface

Operate: scan and organize a personal inbox, then read a message without surrounding noise.

## World

Courrier is a clear, tactile inbox for people who use several personal domains. The broad chronological mail list is the primary surface; the brand menu is the quiet control point for moving between mailboxes, Imbox, The Feed, Paper Trail, Screener and settings. HEY is a workflow reference, not a source of marks or copied assets.

## First viewport

- A slim three-part top bar: search at left, Courrier menu centered, personal profile at right.
- Mailbox views are absent from the default chrome and live inside the Courrier menu.
- The active mailbox title anchors a generous chronological list, with unread mail above previously seen mail in Imbox.
- New senders have one mint screening action; the compose action uses electric violet.

## Palette

- Night violet: `#211936` — primary ink.
- Electric violet: `#5B38F5` — active navigation, focus and primary actions.
- Soft violet: `#F4F1FF` — quiet grouped surface for previously seen mail.
- Mint: `#A6F2DD` — sender-screening action.
- Paper: `#FFFFFF` — reading and list surfaces.
- Lilac line: `#E9E5F2` — structural separators.

## Type and shape

Use a confident sans-serif UI with large, compact mailbox titles and smaller, quieter sender previews. Rows are separated by fine rules; grouped sections use one restrained tinted field rather than repeated cards. The Courrier C mark is the signature brand shape.

## Interaction and states

- The Courrier brand control opens a keyboard-accessible menu for mailbox destinations and settings.
- Keyboard shortcuts remain active without being printed beside navigation items.
- Opening an unread message marks it as read and moves it to the previously-seen section.
- Keep keyboard focus visible, respect reduced motion, and retain light, dark and system themes.
- Local test messages are synthetic and named as test data at the point where they are loaded.

## Responsive behavior

On narrow screens the top bar stays compact, the menu remains the single mailbox switcher, message rows keep subject and sender preview readable, and opening a message uses the full reading surface.
